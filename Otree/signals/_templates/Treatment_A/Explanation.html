In this part, each round will start with the price hint about Period 2. You have to check the hint by clicking on the “Get hint” button so that you can see the matrix with red and blue dots for 8 seconds. Then you must state the probability that you attribute to the majority of dots being red.

Once you confirmed the stated probability that you attribute to the majority of dots being red, you will be informed about your endowment profile and the potential payoffs associated with the various possible consumption choices for both prices. You will also see your own stated belief for P2=2.

After selecting and confirming your consumption choice, the next round starts with a new price hint.

This procedure is repeated for 12 rounds.
