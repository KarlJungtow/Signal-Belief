In this part, each round will start with an information about your endowment profile in this period. You will also see the table with the possible consumption choices and the potential payoffs associated with these choices for both prices. In this part, you have to make your consumption choice before you get the hint for the Period 2 price.

Recall that the probabilities for the price in Period 2 being 0.5 or 2 are 50% each. Thus, both events are equally likely.

After selecting and confirming your consumption choice, you will get the price hint for this round.

Once you stated and confirmed the stated probability that you attribute to the majority of dots being red, the next round starts with an information about next period’s endowment and a new consumption choice.

This procedure is repeated for 12 rounds.
