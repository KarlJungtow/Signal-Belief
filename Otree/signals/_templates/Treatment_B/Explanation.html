In this part, each round starts with information about your endowment profile for that round. You will also see a table with the possible consumption choices and the corresponding payoff points for both possible prices.

In Part 3, you must choose your consumption in Period 1 before you receive the hint about the Period 2 price.

Price uncertainty

Recall that the price of consumption in Period 2, p2, can be:

  • P2 = 0.5, or
  • P2 = 2.

Each of these two prices occurs with probability 50%. Thus, both price levels are equally likely.

Sequence of events within a round

  1. At the beginning of the round, you see your endowment profile for that round and the payoff table for all possible consumption choices at both price levels.
  2. You select and confirm your consumption choice for Period 1.
  3. After confirming your consumption choice, you receive the price hint for that round (the graph with red and blue dots, as explained earlier).
  4. You then state and confirm the probability that you assign to the majority of dots being red.
  5. Once you have confirmed your stated probability, the next round begins with information about the new endowment profile and a new consumption choice.

This procedure is repeated for a total of 12 rounds in Part 3.
